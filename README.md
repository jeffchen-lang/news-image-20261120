A dense one-page infographic summing up Friday 20 November 2026's world news — 25 stories in all. The lead is Russia's massive overnight strike on Ukraine hours after the Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral in Shenzhen, followed by three columns: Markets (the Nov 19 selloff, Anthropic's IPO debut, BTC's slide to ~$61.3K), Conflict & World (APEC's close, the Crimea bridge, the Houthi hit on Riyadh), and Tech & More (Nvidia subpoenaed, the AUDIT AI Act, COP31). `main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory, byte for byte, every run.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```
